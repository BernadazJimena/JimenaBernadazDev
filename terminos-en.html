1. Purpose and acceptance

This document sets out the conditions under which JNB Creative Studio provides design and development services for landing pages and websites, as well as online store design and setup. Requesting a quote, approving a proposal, or starting a project means you fully accept these terms.

2. Services offered

Visual design, layout, and development services for custom landing pages and websites for independent professionals and entrepreneurs, including:

  • Design of the visual identity and page structure.
  • Custom website development hand-coded with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, without templates or content management systems such as WordPress.
  • Basic optimization for mobile devices and search engines.
  • Assistance with publishing the site.

The online store design service is offered exclusively on Tiendanube and Empretienda. Stores on other e-commerce platforms (such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or similar) are not designed. Tiendanube is available in several Latin American countries, while Empretienda operates only in Argentina; the client is responsible for confirming that the chosen platform is available in their country before hiring the service.

For stores built on Tiendanube or Empretienda, the design and customization are limited to the features and options available under the plan the client selects for their store. Each platform sets its own plans, terms, and features, which are outside JNB Creative Studio's control.

The quoted fee for online store design and customization covers one store on one platform, selected by the client: Tiendanube or Empretienda. If stores on both platforms are requested, the additional work will be quoted and charged separately.

The Entrepreneur Plan includes uploading up to 20 (twenty) products and setting up the store (payment methods, shipping, and business details) based on the information, photos, and prices provided by the client. Uploading additional products beyond that limit has an extra cost, quoted according to the number of products and added to the plan price. Identity and account verification with the platform and payment providers must be completed by the store owner, as it requires their personal data and documents. JNB Creative Studio does not request or handle the client's banking passwords or personal documents.

If the client also requests a landing page in addition to the online store design, it will be quoted separately based on the number of sections and the project's complexity.

3. Quotes and payment terms

Plans are priced as published on the website. Add-ons (for example, products beyond the Entrepreneur Plan limit) and projects outside the plans are quoted individually based on their scope and complexity. For contracts arranged outside the online store, a 50% deposit of the total fee is required to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon final delivery.

Purchases made through the online store must be paid in full in a single 100% payment, as the platform does not support partial payments. Payment methods for direct contracts are agreed with the client.

The Basic Plan has an estimated delivery time of 5 (five) business days, counted from receipt of all required material. The Entrepreneur Plan has an estimated delivery time of 7 (seven) business days and the Premium Plan, 15 (fifteen) business days, counted the same way. All timelines depend on receiving content, images, and any other required material from the client on time. Delays in providing this material may change the originally agreed timeline.

The client is responsible for domain registration and renewal fees. These costs are not included in the service quote.

The Premium Plan includes 30 days of support from the site's launch for questions and minor adjustments within the agreed scope. After that period, any update is quoted and charged individually, whenever the client requests it.
