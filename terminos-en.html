1. What these terms are

These terms explain how JNB Creative Studio works. We create landing pages (one-page websites that present your work), websites, and online stores.

If you request a quote, approve a proposal, or we start a project together, you accept these terms.

2. What we offer

We design and build landing pages and websites for independent professionals and entrepreneurs. This includes:

  • The design of the website and the order of its sections.
  • Custom coding with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We don't use templates or WordPress.
  • A website that looks good on phones and is ready to appear in search engines like Google.
  • Help publishing the website online.

Online stores:

  • We only design stores on Tiendanube and Empretienda. We don't work with other platforms, such as Shopify or WooCommerce.
  • Tiendanube works in several Latin American countries. Empretienda only works in Argentina. Before you buy, check that the platform is available in your country.
  • What we can do depends on the plan you choose on Tiendanube or Empretienda. Each platform decides what each of its plans allows.
  • The price covers one store on one platform. If you want stores on both platforms, it costs extra.
  • The Entrepreneur Plan includes uploading up to 20 products and setting up the store: payment methods, shipping, and business details. We use the text, photos, and prices you send us. If you have more than 20 products, the rest cost extra, depending on how many there are.
  • You have to complete the account identity check yourself, because it asks for your personal data and documents. We will never ask for your bank passwords or personal documents.
  • If you also want a landing page, we quote it separately.

3. Prices and payments

  • Plans have the price shown on the website.
  • Extras (for example, more than 20 products) and projects that don't fit a plan are quoted separately.
  • If you hire us directly: you pay 50% to start and the other 50% when we deliver the work. We agree on the payment method together.
  • If you buy through our online store: you pay 100% when you buy, because the store doesn't allow split payments.

4. Delivery times

Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays), starting when you send us all the material: text, photos, and logo.

  • Entrepreneur Plan: 7 business days.
  • Basic Plan: 5 business days.
  • Premium Plan: 15 business days.

If the material arrives late, the delivery will also be late.

5. What we need from you

To meet the delivery times, we need you to:

  • Send us the text, photos, logo, and any access we need (for example, to your domain or hosting) on time.
  • Review and approve our progress within the times we agree on.

The domain is your website address (for example, yourname.com). You register it yourself, in your own name, wherever you prefer (for example, NIC Argentina or GoDaddy). You pay for the domain registration and renewal, and these costs are not included in the price.

6. Adjustments and changes

An adjustment is a change you ask for while we are working on your website or store. Each plan includes:

  • Entrepreneur Plan and Basic Plan: 1 round of adjustments.
  • Premium Plan: 3 rounds of adjustments.

Adjustments must be about what we agreed on at the start. If you want to add something new, we quote it separately.

After we deliver the work, we only make changes if you ask for them, and each change is charged separately. We don't charge a monthly maintenance fee.

The Premium Plan includes 30 calendar days of support after the website goes live. During that time, questions and small changes are free. After that, each change is charged separately.

7. Publishing and hosting

Hosting is the service that stores your website so people can see it online. You can choose between two options:

  • We publish it on Cloudflare. Your website stays on our Cloudflare account and is free for you as long as Cloudflare stays free. If that changes, we will let you know in advance so we can decide together how to continue.
  • You use your own hosting. We give you the website files, ready to upload. If we agree that we will upload it, our work ends when the website is live. If not, it ends when we deliver the files.

The domain is always yours. If you later want to move your website somewhere else, we will send you the files as a .zip or in the format you need.

If you use your own hosting, the server depends on you and your provider: how it works, its security, backups, and costs. We are not responsible for problems with servers we don't manage. If you later need help with that hosting, it is charged as a change.

If you hire other outside services, you pay for them.

8. Who owns the design

Once you finish paying, you can use the design of your website.

We may show the work in our portfolio and on our social media, even if the website is on your own hosting. If you don't want us to show it, let us know and we will agree on it in writing.

  • If you bought through our online store: we start working when you send us all the material and confirm the start. If you cancel before that, we refund everything. If you cancel after that, we refund 50%. The refund is made through the store.

If something unusual happens, we will look at it based on how far the work has progressed. This does not affect your rights as a consumer under the law.
