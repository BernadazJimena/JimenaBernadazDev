Terms and conditions

This page describes how JNB Creative Studio works: what each plan includes, how payments are made, delivery times, and what happens in case of changes or cancellations. By requesting a quote or purchasing a plan, the client accepts these terms.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Summary

  • Plans are priced as published on the website.
  • For direct contracts, a 50% deposit is required to start the work, and the remaining 50% is paid on delivery. Purchases made through JNB Creative Studio's online store are paid in full at the time of purchase.
  • Delivery times start once all the material has been received: 7 business days (Entrepreneur), 5 (Basic), and 15 (Premium).
  • There is no monthly maintenance fee. Updates after delivery are quoted only at the client's request.
  • The domain (the website address) belongs to the client, who is responsible for its cost.
  • For online stores, the client pays for their Tiendanube or Empretienda plan, and the design is limited to what that plan allows.
  • If the project is cancelled after work has started, the 50% deposit is not refunded.
  • JNB Creative Studio may include the project in its portfolio.

1. Purpose and acceptance
  • These terms describe how JNB Creative Studio works. The studio designs landing pages (one-page websites that present a business or service), websites, and online stores.
  • By requesting a quote, approving a proposal, or starting a project, the client accepts these terms.

2. Services offered

JNB Creative Studio designs and builds landing pages and websites for independent professionals and entrepreneurs. The service includes:

  • Visual design of the website and organization of its sections.
  • Custom coding with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, without templates or WordPress.
  • Responsive design for phones and tablets, and basic optimization for search engines such as Google.
  • Assistance with publishing the website.

Online stores

  • Store design is offered only on Tiendanube and Empretienda. Other platforms, such as Shopify or WooCommerce, are not supported.
  • Tiendanube is available in several Latin American countries; Empretienda, only in Argentina. The client must confirm that the platform is available in their country before purchasing.
  • The client chooses and pays for their Tiendanube or Empretienda plan. Its cost is not included in the service price.
  • The store design is limited to the features allowed by the plan the client has on the platform. Each platform defines what each of its plans includes.
  • If a feature requires a higher plan, the client decides whether to upgrade. Otherwise, the design is adapted to what their current plan allows.
  • The price covers one store on one platform. Store design on both platforms is quoted separately.
  • The Entrepreneur Plan includes uploading up to 20 products and setting up the store (payment methods, shipping, and business details), based on the text, photos, and prices provided by the client. Products beyond that limit are quoted separately, depending on the quantity.
  • Account identity verification must be completed by the store owner, as it requires their personal data and documents. JNB Creative Studio does not request bank passwords or personal documents.
  • If the client also wants a landing page, it is quoted separately.

3. Quotes and payment terms
  • Plans are priced as published on the website.
  • Add-ons (for example, more than 20 products) and projects that do not match a plan are quoted separately, based on their scope.
  • Direct contracts: a 50% deposit is required to start the work, and the remaining 50% is paid on final delivery. Payment methods are agreed upon with the client.
  • Purchases through JNB Creative Studio's online store: the full amount is paid at the time of purchase, as the platform does not allow partial payments.

4. Delivery timelines

Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays), starting once all the required material has been received: text, images, and logo.

  • Entrepreneur Plan: 7 business days.
  • Basic Plan: 5 business days.
  • Premium Plan: 15 business days.

Delays in sending the material may change the delivery date.

5. Client responsibilities

To meet the agreed timelines, the client agrees to:

  • Send the text, images, logo, and any necessary access (for example, to the domain or hosting) on time.
  • Review and approve progress within the timelines agreed for each stage.
  • Register the domain in their own name, with the provider of their choice (for example, NIC Argentina or GoDaddy). The domain is the website address, for example, yourname.com. Registration and renewal costs are paid by the client and are not included in the service price.

6. Revisions and changes

An adjustment is any change requested while the project is being developed. Each plan includes:

  • Entrepreneur Plan and Basic Plan: 1 round of adjustments.
  • Premium Plan: 3 rounds of adjustments.
  • Adjustments must stay within the scope agreed at the start of the project. Changes that expand that scope are quoted separately.
  • Once the project has been delivered, updates are made only at the client's request and are quoted per update. JNB Creative Studio does not offer monthly maintenance plans.
  • The Premium Plan includes 30 calendar days of support after the website goes live, for questions and minor adjustments at no cost. After that period, each update is quoted separately.

7. Publishing and hosting

Hosting is the service that stores the website so it can be viewed online. The client can choose between two options:

  • Publishing on Cloudflare by JNB Creative Studio: the website is hosted on JNB Creative Studio's Cloudflare account, at no cost to the client as long as Cloudflare keeps its free plan. If those conditions change, the client will be notified in advance to agree on how to proceed.
  • Client's own hosting: JNB Creative Studio delivers the website files ready to publish. If uploading was agreed upon, the work ends once the website is live; otherwise, it ends with the delivery of the files.
  • The domain always belongs to the client. If the client later wants to move the website, JNB Creative Studio will provide the files as a .zip file or in the format requested.
  • When the website is published on the client's own hosting, its operation, security, backups, and costs are the responsibility of the client and their provider. JNB Creative Studio is not responsible for problems with servers it does not manage. Later assistance with that hosting is quoted as an update.
  • The cost of any third-party services hired by the client is the client's responsibility.

8. Intellectual property
  • Once the project has been paid in full, the client acquires the usage rights to the design of their website.
  • JNB Creative Studio may showcase the project in its portfolio and on its professional social media, even if the website is hosted on the client's own hosting, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

9. Cancellations and refunds
  • Direct contracts: if the client cancels after work has started, the 50% deposit is not refunded, as it covers the hours already worked.
  • Purchases through the online store: work begins once the client sends all the material and confirms the start. If the client cancels before that, the full amount is refunded. If the client cancels afterward, 50% is refunded. Refunds are processed through the store.
  • Exceptional situations will be evaluated case by case, based on the progress of the project. This does not affect the rights granted to consumers by law.

10. Limitation of liability
  • JNB Creative Studio builds the website or store according to the agreed scope.
  • Business results, such as sales, visits, or Google rankings, are not guaranteed, as they depend on factors beyond the design.

11. Communication

  • Messages are answered within 24 business hours. Messages received outside those hours are answered on the next business day.
  • Projects in English are handled in writing only, via WhatsApp chat or email.
  • Inquiries can also be sent through the website's contact form. The data (name, email address, and message) is received by email through the Web3Forms service, is not stored on the website, and is used only to reply to the inquiry. It is not shared or sold. The client may request its deletion through any contact channel.
