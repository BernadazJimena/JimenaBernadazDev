How I work

This is the path from your first message to your published website or online store. You do not need any tech knowledge: I guide you every step of the way.

  1. 1

    You tell me about your project

    Write to me on WhatsApp ↗ or through the contact form and tell me what you do and what you need. If you are not sure which plan suits you, we will figure it out together.

  2. 2

    We choose the plan

    I recommend the plan that best fits your project. You can compare the plans first. If you need something that is not part of any plan, I send you a separate quote.

  3. 3

    You confirm and pay

    If you hire me directly, you pay a 50% deposit to get started. If you buy the plan in my online store, you pay at checkout: in a single payment or by credit card in installments.

  4. 4

    You send me the material

    You fill out a short questionnaire about your project and send me the text, photos, logo, and brand colors. For an online store, also your products with their prices and access to your store. The delivery time starts once I have all the material.

  5. 5

    I design and build your website

    With all the material, I design and code your landing page or custom website, or design and set up your online store. Delivery takes 5 business days (Basic Plan), 7 (Entrepreneur Plan), or 15 (Premium Plan).

  6. 6

    We review it together

    I show you the result, and you tell me what adjustments you want. Depending on the plan, you have one round of adjustments (Basic and Entrepreneur plans) or three (Premium Plan).

  7. 7

    We go live

    For landing pages and custom websites, I guide you to register your domain, we connect it together, and I publish your website on my hosting at no cost. Online stores are ready on Tiendanube or Empretienda. If you hired me directly, the remaining 50% is paid on delivery.

  8. 8

    After launch

    Your website is yours, and there is no monthly fee. If you want to change something later, just ask me, and I quote each update. The Premium Plan includes 30 calendar days of free support for questions and minor adjustments.
