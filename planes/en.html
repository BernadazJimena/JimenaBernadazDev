Entrepreneur Plan · Online store on Tiendanube or Empretienda

$43 USD

For businesses getting started: I design your store on Tiendanube or Empretienda, with your brand's identity.

What it includes

  • Design and customization of your store on Tiendanube (Latin America) or Empretienda (Argentina only).
  • Your brand colors, fonts, and logo applied to the store.
  • 100% mobile-responsive store.
  • Upload of up to 20 products, with names and descriptions written so search engines can find them (basic SEO).
  • Store setup: payment methods, shipping, and business details.
  • One store on one platform, of your choice. Store design on both platforms is quoted separately.
  • One round of adjustments.

What I need from you

  • The text, photos, and prices of your products.
  • Your logo and brand colors.
  • Access to your store on Tiendanube or Empretienda.

Delivery time

  • 7 business days, counted from when I receive all the material.
  • If you add extras or your project is more complex, it may take longer: we agree on the delivery time before starting.

Good to know

  • I only work with Tiendanube (Nuvemshop in Brazil) and Empretienda; I do not design stores on other platforms, such as Shopify or WooCommerce. Tiendanube works in several Latin American countries and Empretienda only in Argentina: before hiring, check that the platform is available in your country.
  • Tiendanube and Empretienda charge their own monthly subscription, which you pay directly to the platform, depending on the plan you choose. That payment does not depend on JNB Creative Studio, and I do not charge anything for it: no commission and no intermediation fee. The Entrepreneur Plan price is a one-time payment and covers only the design and setup of your store.
  • The design and setup of the store (payment methods, shipping, and business details) are limited to what your platform plan allows. If a feature requires a higher plan, you decide whether to upgrade; otherwise, the design is adapted to your current plan.
  • If you have more than 20 products, the additional ones are quoted separately, depending on the quantity.
  • After delivery, I can add new products to your catalog on a regular basis, at your request. Each upload counts as an update and is quoted separately.
  • I handle the store design and the initial product upload. Day-to-day management (stock, prices, orders, shipping, and customer service) is your responsibility.
  • You must complete the account identity verification yourself, as it requires your personal data and documents. I will never ask for your bank passwords or personal documents.

Basic Plan · Essential landing page

$72 USD

For those who need a clear, straightforward online presence ready to share.

What it includes

  • A home page with 5 sections, for example: about, services, testimonials, gallery, and FAQ. In addition, the opening section, a contact section, and a location section (with the Google Maps map) are always included, and they are not counted within the 5. The opening section is the first thing people see: the title, a sentence that says what you do, and a button. The menu and the footer do not count as sections either. If you want to add secondary pages, that is, separate pages (such as one with details about your services), they are quoted separately.
  • If you like, one of your 5 sections can be an availability section with your business hours. The “open / closed” sign, which changes automatically with the time, is not included in this plan: it is an extra that is quoted separately.
  • Designed and coded from scratch for your project. It does not use a database (see the FAQ).
  • 100% responsive design for phones and tablets.
  • Direct WhatsApp contact button.
  • Links to your social media.
  • Fast loading and basic SEO so Google can find your website.
  • One round of adjustments.
  • I guide you to register your domain and connect it to my hosting, where I publish your website at no cost.

What I need from you

  • The text for each section.
  • The photos or images you want to use.
  • Your logo and brand colors.
  • Your contact details, social media, and address (if you want it shown on Google Maps).

Delivery time

  • 5 business days, counted from when I receive all the material.
  • If you add extras or your project is more complex, it may take longer: we agree on the delivery time before starting.

Premium Plan · Custom website

$122 USD

For growing brands that need a complete website: several pages to show everything you do, with a design made just for you.

What it includes

  • Everything included in the Basic Plan.
  • A home page with 6 to 10 sections, depending on what your project needs, for example: about, services, portfolio, testimonials, gallery, and FAQ. In addition, the opening section and the contact, location, and availability sections are always included, and they are not counted within those sections. The menu and the footer do not count either.
  • Up to 4 secondary pages, that is, separate pages such as service details or a guide. Each one can have up to 3 sections. Each extra secondary page is quoted separately.
  • 100% custom visual design tailored to your brand.
  • 3 (three) rounds of adjustments.
  • 30 calendar days of support after launch, for questions and minor adjustments at no cost.
  • Dark mode / light mode toggle. It is a feature, not a section: it goes in the menu.
  • Availability section with the “open / closed” sign based on your business hours. It is already included and does not count as one of your sections: you do not need to ask for it; I only need your business hours.

What I need from you

  • The text for each section.
  • The photos or images you want to use.
  • Your logo and brand colors.
  • Your contact details, social media, and business hours.

Delivery time

  • 15 business days, counted from when I receive all the material.
  • If you add extras or your project is more complex, it may take longer: we agree on the delivery time before starting.
