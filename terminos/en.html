BEFORE WE START

Terms and conditions

This page describes how JNB Creative Studio works: what each plan includes, how payments are made, delivery times, and what happens in case of changes or cancellations.

By requesting a quote or purchasing a plan, the client accepts these terms.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Summary

  • Plans are priced as published on the website. What each plan includes, its delivery time, and its rounds of adjustments are detailed on the Plans page.
  • For direct contracts, a 50% deposit is required to start the work, and the remaining 50% is paid on delivery. Purchases made through JNB Creative Studio's online store are paid at checkout, in a single payment or by credit card in installments, depending on the options available in the store.
  • Delivery times are counted in business days from the receipt of all the material.
  • There is no mandatory monthly fee. Updates after delivery are quoted at the client's request, and monthly support is optional.
  • The domain (the website address) belongs to the client, who is responsible for its cost.
  • If the project is cancelled after work has started, the 50% deposit is not refunded, even if the reason is that the client did not like the result.
  • JNB Creative Studio may include the project in its portfolio.

1. Purpose and acceptance
  • These terms describe how JNB Creative Studio works. The studio designs landing pages (one-page websites that present a business or service), custom websites (sites with several pages), and online stores.
  • By requesting a quote, approving a proposal, or starting a project, the client accepts these terms.
  • What each plan includes (scope, delivery time, rounds of adjustments, and specific conditions) is detailed on the Plans page, which is part of these terms.

2. Services offered

JNB Creative Studio designs and builds landing pages and custom websites, and designs online stores on Tiendanube and Empretienda, for independent professionals and entrepreneurs. The scope of each service depends on the plan purchased, as detailed on the Plans page.

Landing pages and custom websites are built without a database: they do not include user registration, log-in, a panel for the client to edit the content, or information uploaded by visitors. The contact form does work, because it sends messages by email. At this time, JNB Creative Studio does not offer projects that require these features.

3. Quotes and payment terms
  • Plans are priced as published on the website.
  • Add-ons (for example, products beyond the Entrepreneur Plan limit) and projects that do not match a plan are quoted separately, based on their scope.
  • Direct contracts: a 50% deposit is required to start the work, and the remaining 50% is paid on final delivery. Payment methods are agreed upon with the client.
  • Purchases through JNB Creative Studio's online store: the plan is paid at checkout, with no deposit or balance. It can be paid in a single payment or by credit card in installments; the number of installments depends on the options available in the store.

4. Delivery timelines

Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays), starting once all the required material has been received: text, images, and logo. The delivery time of each plan is listed on the Plans page.

Delays in sending the material may change the delivery date.

The published delivery times apply to each plan as described. If the project includes extras or is more complex, the delivery time is agreed with the client before the work begins.

If the client needs delivery by a specific date or sooner than the plan's delivery time, an urgent delivery can be agreed, subject to availability and at an additional cost.

5. Client responsibilities

To meet the agreed timelines, the client agrees to:

  • Send the text, images, logo, and any necessary access (for example, to the domain or hosting) on time.
  • Review and approve progress within the timelines agreed for each stage.
  • Register the domain in their own name, with the provider of their choice (for example, NIC Argentina or GoDaddy). The domain is the website address, for example, yourname.com. Registration and renewal costs are paid by the client and are not included in the service price.

6. Revisions and changes

An adjustment is any change requested while the project is being developed. The number of rounds of adjustments of each plan is listed on the Plans page.

  • Adjustments must stay within the scope agreed at the start of the project. Changes that expand that scope are quoted separately.
  • Once the project has been delivered, updates (for example, changes to text or images) are made only at the client's request and are quoted per update. There is no mandatory monthly fee: optionally, the client may hire monthly support, with no minimum commitment. Monthly support includes questions during business hours and up to 3 updates of existing sections per month; new sections or pages are quoted separately, and unused updates do not carry over.

7. Publishing and hosting

Hosting is the service that stores the website so it can be viewed online. The client can choose between two options:

  • Publishing on Cloudflare by JNB Creative Studio: the website is hosted on JNB Creative Studio's Cloudflare account, at no cost to the client as long as Cloudflare keeps its free plan. If those conditions change, the client will be notified in advance to agree on how to proceed.
  • Client's own hosting: JNB Creative Studio delivers the website files ready to publish. If uploading was agreed upon, the work ends once the website is live; otherwise, it ends with the delivery of the files.
  • The domain always belongs to the client. If the client later wants to move the website, JNB Creative Studio will provide the files as a .zip file or in the format requested.
  • When the website is published on the client's own hosting, its operation, security, backups, and costs are the responsibility of the client and their provider. JNB Creative Studio is not responsible for problems with servers it does not manage. Later assistance with that hosting is quoted as an update.
  • The cost of any third-party services hired by the client is the client's responsibility.

8. Intellectual property
  • Use of the website: once the full project amount has been paid, the client may use their website and its design at no additional cost.
  • Website files: if the client publishes the website on their own hosting, JNB Creative Studio delivers the files in .zip format, and the client may use and modify them for their own website. If the website is published on JNB Creative Studio's hosting, the files are not delivered as part of the service; if the client later decides to move it, they may request them (see section 7).
  • Client material: the text, images, logo, and other material provided by the client remain the client's property. The client confirms that they have the right to use this material, and JNB Creative Studio is not responsible for third-party material provided without authorization.
  • Images and fonts: JNB Creative Studio only uses the client's material and free resources that allow commercial use, such as Google Fonts. It never uses third-party copyrighted material without authorization.
  • Online stores: the platform, its templates, and its features belong to Tiendanube or Empretienda and are governed by their own terms. The store content belongs to the client.
  • Own resources: JNB Creative Studio may reuse its knowledge, methods, and generic pieces of code in other projects (for example, how a menu or the dark mode button works), but never a client's content, brand, or custom design.
  • Portfolio: JNB Creative Studio may showcase the finished project in its portfolio and on its professional social media, regardless of where the website is published or whether the files were delivered, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

9. Cancellations and refunds
  • Direct contracts: if the client cancels after work has started, the 50% deposit is not refunded, as it covers the hours already worked.
  • Purchases through the online store: work begins once the client sends all the material and confirms the start. If the client cancels before that, the full amount is refunded. If the client cancels afterward, 50% is refunded. Refunds are made through the same payment method used for the purchase. If the purchase was paid by credit card in installments, the refund is applied by the bank or card issuer, according to their timelines.
  • If the client is not happy with the result: the rounds of adjustments are there to fix whatever the client does not like. If the client is still not happy after using them, the project can be cancelled. The 50% deposit is not refunded, because it pays for the time already worked, and the rest does not have to be paid. If the plan was bought in the online store, 50% is refunded. The cancelled website is not delivered or published, and the design cannot be used.
  • Exceptional situations will be evaluated case by case, based on the progress of the project. This does not affect the rights granted to consumers by law.

10. Limitation of liability
  • JNB Creative Studio builds the website or store according to the agreed scope.
  • Business results, such as sales, visits, or Google rankings, are not guaranteed, as they depend on factors beyond the design.

11. Communication
  • Support is provided via WhatsApp, Monday to Friday, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. (Argentina time).
  • Messages are answered within 24 business hours. Messages received outside those hours are answered on the next business day.
  • Projects in English and Portuguese are handled in writing only, via WhatsApp chat or email.
  • Inquiries can also be sent through the website's contact form. The information provided (name, email address, and message) is used only to reply to the inquiry and is never shared or sold. The client may ask for it to be deleted at any time.

  • Changes apply from the moment they are published on this page.
