PLANS & SERVICES

What each plan includes

Here you will find the full details of each plan: what it includes, what we need from you, delivery times, and what to keep in mind before hiring.

Entrepreneur Plan · Online Store Design

$43 USD

What it includes

  • Design and customization of your store on Tiendanube (Latin America) or Empretienda (Argentina only).
  • Your brand colors, fonts, and logo applied to the store.
  • 100% mobile-responsive store.
  • Upload of up to 20 products, with names and descriptions written to appear in search engines (basic SEO).
  • Store setup: payment methods, shipping, and business details.
  • One store on one platform, of your choice. Store design on both platforms is quoted separately.
  • One round of adjustments.

What we need from you

  • The text, photos, and prices of your products.
  • Your logo and brand colors.
  • Access to your store on Tiendanube or Empretienda.

Delivery time

  • 7 business days, counted from when we receive all the material.

Good to know

  • We only work with Tiendanube and Empretienda. We do not design stores on other platforms, such as Shopify or WooCommerce.
  • Tiendanube and Empretienda charge their own monthly subscription, which you pay directly to the platform, depending on the plan you choose. That payment does not depend on JNB Creative Studio, and we do not charge anything for it: no commission and no intermediation fee. The Entrepreneur Plan price is a one-time payment and covers only the design and setup of your store.
  • Payment, shipping, and business setup depends on the options included in your platform plan and on the scope of that plan.
  • If you have more than 20 products, the additional ones are quoted separately, depending on the quantity.
  • After delivery, we can add new products to your catalog on a regular basis, at your request. Each upload counts as an update and is quoted separately.
  • We handle the store design and the initial product upload. Day-to-day management (stock, prices, orders, shipping, and customer service) is your responsibility.
  • You must complete the account identity verification yourself, as it requires your personal data and documents.
  • If you also want a landing page in addition to the store, it is quoted separately.

Basic Plan · Essential landing page

$72 USD

What it includes

  • Landing page with 5 (five) basic sections.
  • Hand-coded from scratch, without templates or WordPress.
  • 100% responsive design for phones and tablets.
  • Direct WhatsApp contact button.
  • Links to your social media and your location on Google Maps.
  • Fast loading and basic SEO, to help you appear on Google.
  • One round of adjustments.
  • Support publishing the site and connecting your domain.

What we need from you

  • The text for each section.
  • The photos or images you want to use.
  • Your logo and brand colors.
  • Your contact details, social media, and address (if you want it shown on Google Maps).

Delivery time

  • 5 business days, counted from when we receive all the material.

Good to know

  • You register the domain (your website address) in your own name. Its cost is not included.
  • You can publish the website on Cloudflare, on our account at no cost, or on your own hosting.
  • After delivery, each change is quoted as an update. We do not charge a monthly maintenance fee.
